Privacy Policy

MEDICARE — a dose calculator for pharmacists

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · อ่านฉบับภาษาไทย

This app collects no personal data, transmits nothing, and shares nothing. Everything you enter is calculated on your device and is gone when you close the app.

What the app takes as input

To calculate a dose, the app accepts:

  • Age, body weight, sex, and serum creatinine
  • An assessment of gastrointestinal bleeding risk
  • Paediatric weight and age, for the tuberculosis calculations

These values live in memory only. They are never written to storage, never leave the device, and are discarded as soon as the app closes. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no database.

What the app does not do

  • No internet connection — it runs entirely offline
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs
  • No access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files
  • No patient health information stored on the device or any server
  • No data sold or disclosed to third parties

Permissions

The app requests no operating-system permissions.

Medical disclaimer

MEDICARE is a calculation aid for healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device and does not constitute a prescription or treatment decision.

Results follow the 2021 EHRA Practical Guide on the Use of NOACs in Patients with AF and standard tuberculosis treatment guidelines. Users must always verify against the product label, the current guideline, and the individual patient's context before making any clinical decision.

The developer accepts no liability for outcomes arising from use of this app without professional verification.

Children

This app is intended for healthcare professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the revision published on this page.
